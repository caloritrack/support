Hello! Welcome to your **Profile** section in CaloriTrack. This space is your personal control center, specifically designed for you to customize the application to your needs, keep your health data up to date, manage your subscription, and explore all the educational resources we have prepared to accompany you on your journey toward a healthier life.

Below, we present a step-by-step guide so you can discover and make the most out of every corner of this view.

---

## 📸 1. Your Profile Card (Photo and Name)

At the top of the screen, you will find your full name and your current profile picture.

* **Changing your profile picture:** Want to give it a more personal touch? Simply tap the **camera icon** (located directly over your profile picture circle). This will open your mobile device's media gallery. Upon selecting an image, you will access an integrated editor where you can crop it into a perfect square so it looks amazing. Once saved, your photo will synchronize automatically across the entire ecosystem.

## ⚙️ 2. Section: Your Account

Here you will find all the tools to customize the technical experience of your application and safeguard your data security.

### 🎨 Settings (Appearance & Language)
* **Theme:** Tap this option to choose whether you prefer the app to display in **Light Mode** ☀️, **Dark Mode** 🌙, or automatically adapt to your phone's global system settings.
* **Language:** Change the application's language at any time between **Spanish**, **English**, or your device's default language.

### 👤 Personal Data
Here you can update the baseline information that our algorithm uses to calculate your personalized caloric and macronutrient requirements. You can modify:
* Full name.
* Date of birth.
* Gender.
* Main body metrics: **Height (cm)** and **Weight (kg)**.

:::tip[Important Saving Note!]
Do not forget to press the **"Save"** button in the upper right corner after making any changes to your data to ensure that new metabolic calculations are applied immediately.
:::

### 🔑 Change Password
If you created your account using a traditional email and password, you can update it here for security purposes.
* The system will prompt you for your current password and guide you to create a new one.
* To be valid, it must be secure: minimum of **8 characters**, at least **one uppercase letter**, and **one special character**.
* We will show you a real-time dynamic checklist (**✅**) to ensure your new password complies with all safety guidelines.

### 🔔 Notification Settings
Stay in absolute control of the reminders you wish to receive. In this section, you can turn on or off CaloriTrack's alerts and push notifications on your device, ensuring you never miss important notices regarding your hydration, fasting, or meal habits.

### 🔗 Health Synchronization (Apple Health)
Get the most out of your smart devices! Here you can connect CaloriTrack natively with your iPhone's **Health** app.
* We will transparently inform you which data we read (physical activity, sleep analysis, heart rate) and which data we write (nutrition logs, body measurements, moods).

:::info[Synchronization Troubleshooting]
If you notice that a completed workout or a health log is not showing up on your central dashboard, a **"Force Resync"** button is available. This command will scan, search, and retroactively recover all your data from the last **35 days**.
*(Note: Direct integration with Garmin watches is currently marked as "coming soon").*
:::

### 💳 Membership
Your direct pass to the Premium level. Here you can check the real-time status of your subscription: **Active**, **Free Trial**, **Expired**, or **Free Plan**.
* You will be able to see exactly which plan you have contracted and the precise expiration or auto-renewal date.
* If your account is on the basic tier, you will see shortcuts to **Get Premium** and the **Restore Purchases** button in case you have changed your mobile device.

### 🚪 Log Out
Allows you to log out of your account completely and securely on your current device. We will display a quick confirmation window before proceeding to prevent accidental logouts.

### 🗑️ Delete Account
:::danger[Danger Zone: Information Erasure]
If you decide that you want to permanently delete all your traces and data from our servers, you can use this red button. For **extreme security reasons**, the system will request a double confirmation of identity and require you to enter your current password before processing the total and irreversible deletion of your information.
:::
