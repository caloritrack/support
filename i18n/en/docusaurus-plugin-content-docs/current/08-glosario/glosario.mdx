## 🥑 Nutrition and Metabolism Concepts

### Ketosis
A completely natural metabolic state where your body, lacking enough available carbohydrates to use for energy, begins to burn **fats** (both dietary fats and stored body fat) to convert them into highly efficient fuel called ketones.

### KetoFlame
:::tip[Exclusive CaloriTrack Concept]
This is the specific name we use in the application to track your active streak in a state of ketosis. When you maintain consecutive lifestyle habits that keep your body burning fat as energy, you will see your *KetoFlame* light up in the app. It is the visual indicator that your metabolism is firing on all cylinders!
:::

### Macronutrients (or "Macros")
These are the nutrients your body requires in large daily quantities to function properly, sustain energy, and repair tissues. They are divided into three primary groups:
* **Proteins:** The building blocks for muscles and organs (meat, fish, eggs, legumes).
* **Healthy Fats:** Clean metabolic fuel and hormone regulators (avocado, olive oil, nuts).
* **Carbohydrates:** Fast-absorbing or complex energy sources (vegetables, fruits, tubers, grains).

### Antinutrients
Natural compounds found primarily in the outer layers of plants, seeds, grains, and legumes (such as phytates or lectinas). They are named so because they can bind to minerals in your digestive tract, temporarily interfering with the proper absorption of nutrients. At CaloriTrack, we teach you simple techniques (like soaking or correct cooking) to neutralize them.

## 🕒 Lifestyle Science & Biohacking

### Circadian Rhythms
The internal 24-hour biological clock running in the background of your brain. It automatically regulates your sleep-wake cycles, hormone production, digestion, and body temperature. It is heavily influenced by sunlight, which is why CaloriTrack helps you synchronize your meals and rest periods with daylight to optimize energy.

### Intermittent Fasting
Not a diet, but an eating pattern that splits your day into two clear blocks: a **fasting window** (where you consume no calories, allowing your cells to clean and repair themselves) and an **eating window** (the timeframe where you consume your daily meals). It is a powerful tool to enhance your metabolic flexibility.

## 🧘 Wellness and Mental Well-being

### Soundhealing
A relaxation practice that utilizes specific sound frequencies and acoustic vibrations (such as Tibetan singing bowls or binaural beats) to guide your brainwaves into deep meditative states, effectively lowering cortisol levels (the stress hormone) and improving sleep quality. You can listen to these sessions directly in the Wellness tab.

### Breathwork
Refers to any type of conscious, controlled breathing exercise. By deliberately changing the rhythm, depth, or speed of your inhalations and exhalaciones, you send direct signals to your nervous system to instantly relax, lower your heart rate, or boost mental focus.
